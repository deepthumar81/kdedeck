# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want KDE Deck to be a welcoming project for people with different
backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. Everyone who participates in
the project is expected to help keep it respectful, constructive, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements.
- Give actionable feedback focused on the work.
- Welcome questions from people who are learning.
- Respect privacy and do not publish personal information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith participation.
- Publishing private information or credentials.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional open-source
  community.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, reject
contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's GitHub
contact or security-reporting channel rather than escalating them in a public
thread.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a
person who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant and applies to
all project spaces.
